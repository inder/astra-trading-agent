## What real bars showed (SOXL / RKT / IBM, 2026-10-05, private Yahoo minute and daily bars)

With the current defaults, the private real-bar test fails for SOXL:
1. **Runaway gate:** SOXL's 63-session high is 204.55 (Jul 9); Oct 5 was a recovery run 20% below it, so
   `maxBelowHighFraction` 0.10 rejects the founder's own example. (Moot once the gate is off by default.)
2. **Contraction:** with the gate bypassed, the 9:46 box is NOT found: second-half mean range ÷ mean of the 5 candles
   before the box = **0.75**, over the 0.7 limit. At 0.8 the scan finds **box 9:46–10:14, 158.08–160.22, decision up on
   the candle that closes at 10:16**, matching the founder's reading.
3. **Spurious boxes:** the scan also found 5 midday boxes (0.04–0.06 ATR). Three cite a support ~4 points below the box
   (`supportReachAtr` 0.5 ≈ 5.8 points on SOXL is too generous); two cite an anchored VWAP ABOVE the box (164.3 over a
   163.2–163.9 box), allowed by `supportSlackAtr` 0.1. A level above the box is resistance, not support.
4. **Decision timing:** the original brief said "first 2-minute close above the box at 10:14 (160.71)". That was the
   candle STARTING 10:14; it closes at **10:16**. The synthetic fixture encodes "ends 10:14": align it to reality.
